Delivered = sent from the main store. Pulled out = returned to the main store. Damaged = spoiled or broken at the branch. Net = Delivered − Pulled out − Damaged. Amount carried = Delivered × Price. Bread that passes inspection in Bakery Production shows under Available for delivery; every receipt deducts from it. Production issues a delivery receipt for every trip, each with its own number (DR-00001, DR-00002…). A wrong receipt can be voided: it stays on the list but is not counted. Changing a price on a new delivery also sets it for later deliveries; saved receipts keep their price.
