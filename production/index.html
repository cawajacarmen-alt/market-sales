Day summary by bread

Each order goes through 4 steps: Ordered (assigned to a baker), Baking (baker started), Counted (production team counts the yield output), Inspected (after baking, pieces that pass quality inspection are released for delivery; damaged pieces are not released). Expected = batches × yield per batch from Breads & bakers.

Released is the pieces that passed quality inspection in Production orders. Went out is what Branch Deliveries shows as delivered to branches plus the bread in that day's online orders (cancelled orders not counted). Short means the bakery released more than reached the branches and online orders. Over means more went out than the bakery released. Left at bakery = Made − Damaged − Went out. Pull-outs and damage at the branches are shown for reference but do not change the tally.
